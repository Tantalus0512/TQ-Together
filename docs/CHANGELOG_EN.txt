TQ Together - changelog
=======================

1.8.3
- Fixed: souls that fight with a weapon (swords, bows - 91 of 379) hit much harder than intended, some harder than
  the hero. A soul's damage was measured by its bare-hand hit, which most of them do not have, so the corridor did
  not hold them; the pace with the hero was not made up for either - the hand hit was lightened, not the weapon.
  Now the hit is measured by the soul's average weapon on Legendary, every soul has the same corridor (a hero monster
  no stronger than the average boss soul, about 30% of the hero's damage; a boss at most 1.5 times that), and the
  hero's pace is made up for by the soul's damage factor. Example: the Faceless One hit about 50% of the hero's damage
  with its basic attack alone (plus its weapon skills), now about 21%.
- The common soul damage factor is a little lower (x35 instead of x41 for a hero with 75k damage per second): the
  average boss soul now counts the bosses with weapons as well.

1.8.2
- The ordinary chests of the Tartarus arenas (common, infrequent and rare) give a soul shard of any boss or hero
  monster: 10% per opening (SoulTartarusChance=), bosses and heroes equally - half the chance for one of the 78
  bosses, half for one of the 301 heroes. The purple prizes of Tartarus' reward orbs are left alone. Chests of 1-3
  items got an extra item, as the orbs did in 1.8.1; the common ones (0-1 item, nearly all potions) give 20% fewer
  potions.
- The Tartarus soul from his reward orb: exactly 15% per orb on the 3rd cycle (was 15% for EVERY item of the orb).
  The game sets that orb's item count by the arena cycle, so on Legendary it is now about 3% / 8% / 15% / 24% / 34%
  per orb on cycles 1-5 (was 6% / 16% / 28% / 42% / 57%), and the orb's purple items are 8% fewer than vanilla
  instead of 15%.

1.8.1
- Orbs and chests give their vanilla loot again. Every item of an orb is a draw of its own, so a shard took the place
  of a vanilla item: electrum orbs gave 9.5% less of everything else, the Aesir chests 7-10%. Now they hold just
  enough more items that the vanilla ones are as many as in the game on average: an orb gives 8-10 items instead of
  8-9 (vanilla x0.97), the Aesir chests x1.00. The game rounds the item count, so the chests of Thrym, Mimir and Loki
  keep a 2-3% difference. Tartarus' reward orb has no item count to raise: a soul shard still takes the place of its
  item in 15% of openings.
- The per-fight soul chance is counted for each fight. The Exhumed Medjai (three level bands that the developers all
  gave level 72) dropped his soul at 5.3% instead of 15%, the blueprint keeper bandit and the lone Azghar Ha'kor at
  6.2% instead of 12%, Ssaquath and the high priest (two different quests) at 6.2% too. Hades' and Charon's forms,
  Tiamat's stages and the four honor guards still count as one fight.
- Fixed: an item named twice in one loot slot keeps its chance (the Medjai's and four other monsters' health potion
  dropped at 1% instead of 5%).
- The item count of orbs and chests is now worked out as the game does it (rounded, not cut off), so the shard
  chances are exactly the ones set.
- Safer install:
  - texts are put together in a copy of the archive and swapped into the game in one step - a window closed in the
    middle no longer leaves a cut-off Text_XX.arc in the game;
  - a damaged install state file stops neither a start nor the uninstall;
  - a stale GamePath in TQTogether.ini: the game is searched for; a path typed in is added to the ini, the other
    settings stay;
  - a game or Documents folder with square brackets in its name ("... [GOG]") no longer breaks the texts;
  - texts that did not go in (no ArchiveTool.exe) are tried again on the next start instead of "nothing changed";
  - an uninstall without the saved originals says which files to restore with Steam's "Verify integrity";
  - a typo in a number in TQTogether.ini gives a warning and the default instead of a crash; a broken tweak names
    its file and operation.

1.8.0
- A soul from an electrum orb: 20% per orb instead of 10% (SoulOrbChance=20) - about 4 shards in 20 orbs on average,
  and nothing at all about once in 100 (was 2 shards and 12%).

1.7.9
- Electrum orbs give the souls of their land's hero monsters (10% per orb), and boss souls come only from the bosses
  themselves and their chests: bosses are mightier than heroes. Only five bosses with no loot of their own stay in the
  orbs (Baldr, the three Chaos bosses, Toxeanubis), or their souls could not be had. Zazamankh and the Celestial
  Accord now give their souls from their chests (before: from the orbs only). The Eternal Embers heroes are sorted
  into their lands: the Jade Emperor's and Thebes.

1.7.8
- Fixed: soul shards never dropped from electrum orbs and boss chests, and from the orbs of Hades, Tiamat and the
  Jade Emperor neither did eidolon shards. The game reads only six entries per loot slot, ours went in as the
  seventh to ninth. Now all of ours is one entry (a shared sub-table, the same odds inside), and the self-check counts
  the odds by the game's own rules. A soul shard 10% per orb, an eidolon shard 40%, every other item as before.

1.7.7
- The souls' AI no longer shakes the air: waves (Talos' flamethrower, the Chimera's breath) and blasts around the
  caster are used only at distances they reach, not beyond (27 skills of various souls). A skill wired into the AI
  twice for the same distances keeps one slot, the longest delay (7 souls): Talos no longer flames every second, nor
  the Chimera breathes. The bosses themselves do so in the game.

1.7.6
- A dead soul's body dissolves like an eidolon's instead of staying on the ground (Arachne, Nessus, Talos and 278
  more souls whose bosses just fall or vanish only on their fight script's cue). Their own death effects (Cerberus,
  Apophis) are untouched.

1.7.5
- Souls strike and cast at your hero's pace: his attack and cast speed bonus from gear and skills speeds the soul's
  strikes, spells and skills up by as much, but no more than 2 times the boss's own (SoulSpeedMax), so the animations
  do not break. The damage per second stays as it was: the hand hit is lighter by as much, skills hit as before but
  wind up faster. With a hero at +160% attack and +32% cast speed Cerberus bites every 0.6 s instead of 1.2.
- Soul projectiles fly 10% / 20% faster on Epic / Legendary, like bosses' and monsters' (the game gives pets none).

1.7.4
- A boss soul gets the boss's own defence back (boss_conversionimmunity, 69 of 78 bosses): immune to stun, freeze,
  sleep, fear, confusion, conversion and slow, 65-85% resistance to percent-of-life damage, dodge and +50% life on
  Legendary. Before, it went out together with the boss damage bonuses, and the sirens of Atlantis put Talos to
  sleep, slowed and converted him. The damage bonuses are still out: a soul's damage comes from the hero.
- The hero difficulty (Difficulty=power / AutoLife=1) makes the monsters' blows on Legendary HERO_DAMAGE times stronger
  and more accurate (3.25 for a party of three well-geared heroes). The souls now get as much more life and defence
  on Legendary, so a monster's blow lands on a soul as in vanilla Legendary: Cerberus has about 228,000 life instead
  of 70,000. In the "party" difficulty the monsters hit as in the game, and nothing changes there.

1.7.3
- Soul speed cap: no animation of a soul (run, strike, spell, skill) plays more than 2 times faster than the boss's
  own (SoulSpeedMax=2). With auras and buffs on top (Heart of Oak: +10% to every ally) the animations no longer turn
  into mush. Before, only the run had a cap (2.6): Talos' spells played 2.9 times faster, Porcus' skill 4.5. Some
  souls slow by design are a bit slower now (Tartarus 5.4 m/s, Talos 4.6) - the rest of the way they teleport like
  every pet.

1.7.2
- Ormenos no longer tries to throw stalactites by telekinesis: the throw works only in his obsidian cave, elsewhere
  the AI wasted almost half of its turns on it. The stalactites still fall from above and hit enemies.

1.7.1
- Soul damage follows your hero: the average boss soul's basic attack deals 30% of the hero's damage per second
  from the character window (SoulDamage=30), the soul's skills grow by the same factor. The hardest hitters are
  held: a boss up to 45% of the hero's damage, a hero monster up to 30%. Before, a soul dealt 1-3% of a geared
  hero's damage and could do next to nothing on the 4-player difficulty.
- Souls are twice as tough: the average boss soul has about 47,000 life on Legendary (24,000 before), Cerberus about
  70,000.
- Soul speed follows your hero: a soul runs as fast as he does, his run bonus counted. Giants (Typhon, Tartarus, the
  Hydra, the Ancient One and other big bosses) run no faster than they do in the game, and no slower than 7 m/s.
- Fixed: 106 souls (Hades, the Ancient One, Cerberus and more) are animated from an animation table, but their speed
  was measured and changed on dead fields of their own record. Hades and Megalesios were slow because of it, the
  Ancient One a bit too fast. Each such soul now has its own copy of the table.
- Removed skills that work only on the boss's own arena: the poison geysers from the floor cracks of Cerberus and the
  Chaos Cerberus, the geysers of Charon and the Omega Telkine, the turrets of Loki and Surtr.
- Tiamat and the Chaos Apophis Serpent (they cannot walk) spit poison from afar instead of a strike on an 8 s
  cooldown, and teleport to the hero beyond 14 m. Before, Tiamat stood still after her wave while she was hit, and
  Apophis neither attacked nor followed.
- Souls of the heroes from the developers' hidden dungeon carry their names as the game shows them: "Toxeanubis the
  Slayer", "Ari the Bear", "Anthony the Drunken Warrior" (was "toxeanubistheslayer").

1.6.9
- Fixed: bosses slow by design (the Ancient One of Atlantis: run speed 0.3) stayed slow as souls - their run animation
  was sped up beyond what the game accepts. An animation is now sped up no more than monsters have in the game itself,
  the rest comes from the soul's run speed, and a soul runs at most 2.6 times faster than the boss itself: a giant
  stays a giant (the Ancient One about 2 m/s; beyond that it teleports to you like any pet).
- Fixed: a soul drops nothing when it dies (the Ancient One's soul dropped the quest item Magic Compass).

1.6.6
- Enslaved boss souls: every boss (78) and hero monster (301) drops "Enslaved Soul: [name]" - a relic of 3
  shards for a boss, 2 for a hero. The completed soul fits an amulet and summons that boss as a pet companion, at
  your hero's level. It drops from the boss itself (15% per fight, heroes 12%), from the chests of bosses that drop
  nothing themselves (15%) and from the electrum orbs of its land (10%). Every other item keeps its chance.
  Details: README, "Enslaved boss souls".
- A soul counts as a companion for the "party" difficulty.
- A soul leaves no boss chest when it dies. Slow giants (Tartarus and others) as souls run, strike and cast no
  slower than Arachne: speed is measured on the game's own animations (and cast speed), only what is slower is
  sped up.
- The Tartarus soul also drops from his reward orb: in the arena, loot dropped by monsters vanishes.
- Faster database build: scanning thousands of monster records no longer repacks them all.

1.5.1
- Bosses drop eidolon shards themselves when they die - 20% per kill (EidolonBossChance=), Nessus, Arachne, the
  Telkines, the Hydra, Charon and about two hundred more - instead of 10% from their chests in 1.5.0. Boss chests
  are vanilla again; the bosses' own loot is unchanged.

1.5.0
- Eidolon shards drop on every difficulty now: electrum orbs of Normal, Epic and Legendary (40% per orb) and boss
  chests - first and repeat kills of every act boss, plus the boss orbs of Eternal Embers (10% per opening).
  Ordinary chests are untouched; every other item keeps its original chance. EidolonOrbChance / EidolonBossChance
  in TQTogether.ini change the rates (0 = off).
- Fixed: the orb odds counted one item per orb, but an orb gives 7-8 - the advertised "10% per orb" was about 38%
  in fact. Chances are now computed per opening with the engine's own loot rules.

1.4.0
- New default difficulty, "party": every companion your hero can summon (a completed eidolon in a worn ring)
  counts as one more player, and monsters, champions and bosses get the game's own co-op scaling for that party
  size - on Normal, Epic and Legendary (Legendary: +50% monster life per companion, bosses +100%).
  CompanionPlayers=1.5 / 2 in TQTogether.ini makes it harder, 0.5 easier.
- The previous damage-based scaling stays as Difficulty=power for heroes geared far beyond the usual curve;
  older ini files with AutoLife=1 keep working unchanged. Difficulty=off = vanilla monsters.
- TQTogether.ini.example: every line commented out (renaming it no longer sets a wrong game path).

1.3.5
- A healer companion now heals the other companions too: in Titan Quest an AI heals an ally only when that ally
  calls for help while being hit, and companions inherited the doppelganger's limit of 4 calls per summon,
  every 10 s, within 10 m. Now: unlimited, every 2 s, within 20 m; allies are healed below 80% life.
  (The hero has always been healed below 85%.)

1.3.4
- Fixed: a companion's eidolon could vanish from a ring (still in the save, ignored by the game) when two of
  your heroes had non-Latin names of the same length. The database no longer contains hero names; the build
  refuses to install a database the game could misread. No save editing needed - the eidolon comes back.

1.3.3
- Damage per second for the difficulty now matches the character window exactly (attack animation length
  is part of the attack speed shown there).

1.3.2
- Self-check fixes found by building companions for every mastery pair and every item in the game:
  enemy-slowing auras (Deathchill, Plague's Fatigue) no longer count as the companion's own speed; an item proc
  without a free slot is dropped instead of failing the build; taunt skills from items are not transferred;
  harmless vanilla data errors no longer block the build.
- More accurate damage per second: modifiers of passive and toggled skills are counted.

1.3.1
- Fixed: a hero wearing the Hou Yi bow (a vanilla record with a broken sound reference) stopped the build.

1.3.0
- Automatic difficulty (now Difficulty=power) measures damage per second from the save, as the character window
  shows it, instead of the biggest hit ever.

1.2.0
- Companions take damage like the hero: the game's pet bonuses (defense, life, resistances on Legendary), the
  hero's pet-boosting quest rewards and the doppelganger's leftovers are cancelled; racial profile removed.

1.1.x
- Companions use their skills like the hero: up to 5 attacks, toggles, buffs, one chained attack; no taunt;
  pauses between spammable attacks; item auto-cast skills become the companion's own skills.
- Healer companions heal the hero first.
- Optional difficulty settings: Life.<hero>, AutoLife (damage based), AutoLifeBoss, AutoDamagePower, PartyBurden.

1.0.x
- Companions wear the hero's full gear with affixes, relics, amulet and artifact baked in.
- Attack, cast and run speed and their caps are the hero's, not the doppelganger's.
- Database tweaks (JSON files in the data folder) applied under the companions.
- Deleted heroes leave "Faded Eidolons" so saves stay valid; reliable recognition of the installed database.

1.0.0
- First release.
